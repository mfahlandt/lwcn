🛡️ Cloud Native projects released security updates addressing the eMudhra CA compromise.
- Crossplane v2.4.3+ and Cilium v1.20.4+ upgraded Go to distrust eMudhra intermediate CAs.
- Capsule v0.15.0 fixed an authorization bypass (GHSA-p8j8-x28r-3c9v).
- Atlantis v0.49.0 removed Terraform/OpenTofu binaries, requiring user-provided binaries.
